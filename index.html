05 · The workshop

Machinesthat dreamin wire.

From drawing to machine · four stages

Every machine I make begins as a drawing: a few bright points and the thinnest lines between them. Then the points become lights, the lines become wires, and the drawing slowly learns to move.

Each one is modelled, lit and animated piece by piece, then left to loop quietly, like something alive behind glass. They are not made to work. They are made to watch, listen and glow.
